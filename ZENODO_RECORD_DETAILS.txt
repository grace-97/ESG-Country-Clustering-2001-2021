Title:
Country-Level Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) Data Supporting Machine Learning Clustering (2001–2021)

Description:
This repository supports the study "Clustering Countries Based on ESG Metrics: A Machine Learning Approach to ESG Factors." The study uses a compilation of country-level Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) indicators obtained from the World Bank. The researchers report an analysis period of 2001–2021. The data were supplied to an ESG-based classifier prototype to examine preprocessing and clustering using K-means and Hierarchical clustering.

The deposit preserves the supplied original CSV data and accompanying country, indicator and footnote metadata. The original source file contains annual columns outside the 2001–2021 analysis period; those additional years are retained as part of the original download and are not asserted to have been used in the reported experiments. See README.md for details and limitations.
